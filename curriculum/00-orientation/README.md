# Stage 00: Orientation

## Purpose

This stage helps a beginner answer the first important question: what is power electronics, and why should I care?

A learner should leave this stage with a map of the field, not a pile of formulas.

## Core Questions

- What is power electronics?
- Why do we convert electric power from one form to another?
- What are AC, DC, voltage, current, power, and energy in practical systems?
- Where do converters appear in daily life and industry?
- What kinds of people work in this field?
- What does a beginner need to learn first?

## Must-Know Ideas

- Power electronics is about controlling and converting electrical energy efficiently.
- The main conversion types are DC-DC, AC-DC, DC-AC, and AC-AC.
- Switching is the central trick that enables high efficiency.
- Applications include chargers, server power supplies, solar inverters, battery systems, electric vehicles, motor drives, UPS systems, and grid interfaces.
- The field sits between circuits, control, devices, magnetics, thermal design, embedded systems, and safety.

## First Projects

- Find five power electronic converters in your home or school.
- Draw a block diagram of a phone charger, laptop adapter, or EV charging chain.
- Ask an AI tutor to explain the difference between energy conversion and signal processing.
- Create a one-page map of the power electronics industry.

## Exit Criteria

Before moving on, the learner should be able to explain:

- why power converters are needed
- what switching converters do
- what makes power electronics different from ordinary electronics
- why efficiency, heat, size, cost, and safety matter
